# The Sedjem name and logo

The name "Sedjem", the ear icon and the wordmark ([the logo files](docs/brand/README.md)) are trademarks of Mohammed
El-sayed Ahmed, the author of Sedjem. The code's licence, the GNU Affero General Public License, covers the code. It
gives no right to use the name or the logo, so this page says what you may do with them.

The point is that people can trust that a program called Sedjem comes from its author, or with the author's
permission.

## You may, without asking

- Use the name to talk about Sedjem: in an article, a review, a talk, a tutorial or a list of software.
- Say that your project is based on Sedjem, works with it or was made from its code, in plain words, as long as it
  doesn't look like Sedjem itself or like a product its author supports.
- Share an unchanged copy of an official release or of the source, free of charge, with its name and logo.
- Use the logo to link to the project, following [the logo guide](docs/brand/README.md).

## You need written permission

- To use the name or the logo for a changed version. A fork that is changed gets a name and an icon of its own.
- To use the name or the logo for a product or a service that you sell, or that you host for others, including an
  unchanged copy.
- To use the name or the logo in the name of a company, a product, a domain, a social media account or an app store
  listing.
- To use the name or the logo in a way that suggests the author made, checks or supports your product.

Ask for permission through a contact on [the author's GitHub profile](https://github.com/MohammedEl-sayedAhmed). An
answer in writing is the only permission.

## What stays the same

This page doesn't change the licence of the code. Under the AGPL you may still use, study, change and share the code,
and a changed version keeps the author credit that [NOTICE](NOTICE) asks for. Only its name and its logo change.
